================================================================================
               ACADEMIC GUIDELINES — SAMPLE DOCUMENT
================================================================================

1. ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE STANDARDS
---------------------------------
- Students are expected to maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.0 (on 10-point scale).
- Students falling below CGPA 5.0 for two consecutive semesters will be
  placed on academic probation.
- Students on academic probation must attend mandatory counselling sessions.

2. REMEDIAL CLASSES
-------------------
- Remedial classes are offered for students scoring below 50% in any subject.
- Schedule: Extra classes held on Saturdays, 10 AM — 1 PM.
- Attendance in remedial classes is mandatory for eligible students.
- Topics covered: Fundamentals review, problem-solving workshops, and
  doubt-clearing sessions.
- Faculty mentors are assigned to each weak student for personalized guidance.

3. IMPROVEMENT EXAMINATIONS
----------------------------
- Students who have passed but wish to improve their grades may appear for
  improvement exams in the subsequent semester.
- Maximum of 3 subjects can be attempted for improvement per semester.
- The better of the two scores (original or improvement) will be considered.
- Fee for improvement exam: Rs. 500 per subject.

4. WEAK STUDENT IDENTIFICATION CRITERIA
----------------------------------------
- A student is classified as "academically weak" if:
  * Average marks across all subjects < 50%, OR
  * Attendance percentage < 60%, OR
  * Failed in 2 or more subjects in a single semester.
- Interventions for weak students:
  * Mandatory remedial classes
  * Bi-weekly mentor meetings
  * Peer tutoring program (pairing with high performers)
  * Progress tracking with monthly reports to parents/guardians

5. SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT
-------------------------------
- Regular study schedule: Minimum 2 hours of self-study daily.
- Active participation in class discussions and group projects.
- Utilize college resources: library, online databases, lab facilities.
- Seek help early: approach faculty during office hours for doubts.
- Form study groups with classmates for collaborative learning.
- Practice previous years' question papers for exam preparation.
- Maintain a healthy work-life balance: exercise, sleep, nutrition.

6. BRANCH-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES
------------------------------
- CSE: Focus on programming practice (minimum 2 coding problems/day).
  Participate in hackathons and open-source contributions.
- ECE: Emphasize lab work and circuit design projects. Join electronics
  clubs for hands-on experience.
- ME: Focus on workshop skills and CAD software proficiency. Participate
  in design competitions and internships.
- CE: Emphasize site visits and practical knowledge. Study building codes
  and structural analysis thoroughly.

7. MENTORSHIP PROGRAM
---------------------
- Every student is assigned a faculty mentor at the start of each year.
- Mentor meetings: At least once per month (mandatory).
- Mentors track academic progress, attendance, and overall well-being.
- Students can request a change of mentor through the Dean's office.
