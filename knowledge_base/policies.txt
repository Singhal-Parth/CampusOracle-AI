================================================================================
                    COLLEGE POLICIES — OFFICIAL DOCUMENT
================================================================================

1. ATTENDANCE POLICY
--------------------
- Minimum required attendance: 75% per semester.
- Students with attendance below 75% will receive a warning notice.
- Students with attendance below 60% will be marked as "detained" and may
  not be allowed to sit for end-semester examinations.
- Medical leave of up to 10 days per semester is allowed with valid
  documentation from a registered medical practitioner.
- Students with attendance between 60-75% may apply for condonation with
  a fine of Rs. 500 per subject.

2. EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY
--------------------------
- Students must have a minimum of 75% attendance to be eligible for
  end-semester examinations.
- Students must have submitted all mandatory assignments and lab work.
- Students with pending disciplinary actions may be barred from exams.
- Re-examination is allowed once per subject per semester with a fee of
  Rs. 1000.

3. GRADING POLICY
-----------------
- Internal Assessment: 30% (assignments, quizzes, mid-term)
- End-Semester Exam: 70%
- Minimum passing marks: 40% in each subject
- Grade Scale:
  * 90-100: O (Outstanding)
  * 80-89:  A+ (Excellent)
  * 70-79:  A (Very Good)
  * 60-69:  B+ (Good)
  * 50-59:  B (Above Average)
  * 40-49:  C (Average / Pass)
  * Below 40: F (Fail)

4. DISCIPLINARY POLICY
----------------------
- Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable by expulsion.
- Use of mobile phones during lectures and exams is prohibited.
- Damage to college property will result in a fine and possible suspension.
- Academic dishonesty (cheating, plagiarism) will result in zero marks for
  that examination and a disciplinary hearing.

5. FEE POLICY
-------------
- Tuition fees must be paid before the start of each semester.
- Late fee payment attracts a penalty of Rs. 100 per day.
- Scholarships are available for students with:
  * Academic excellence (top 10% of class)
  * Financial need (income certificate required)
  * Sports achievements (state/national level)

6. LIBRARY POLICY
-----------------
- Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time.
- Borrowing period: 14 days, renewable once.
- Late return fine: Rs. 5 per day per book.
- Reference books and journals cannot be borrowed overnight.
